# Simple-camera-systems

Small camera systems I made for a professional project in Unreal Engine.

This camera was for something very similar to a level editor, where the player could control the camera in different ways. The camera could move to a specific location / rotation for the player in some instances, else, the player would either control it by orbiting around an object or freely with no restrictions.

ObjectDropperCamera is the camera actor itself, acting a little like the "manager" for the different camera modes.

ObjectDropperCameraMode are the different camera modes, keeping everything encapsulated from the camera actor.
